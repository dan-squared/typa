# Typa
*The adaptive typing trainer for macOS.*

Typa is a native macOS application designed for those who want to improve their typing through intention, not just repetition. It’s a quiet, focused tool that prioritizes your progress, your privacy, and a premium aesthetic.

## A more intentional way to type
Most typing apps simply measure your speed. Typa is built to help you build it. It focuses on the "spaces between the keys"—the transitions where muscle memory is actually formed.

### What makes Typa unique?
- **It learns as you type.** Instead of picking random sentences, Typa analyzes every keystroke and hesitation. It identifies the specific "digraphs" (key pairs) where you lose rhythm and builds your next lesson around them.
- **Calm, focused design.** Built exclusively for macOS, Typa is designed to feel like a premium part of your desktop. No browser tabs, no ads—just a beautiful, highly customizable space to practice.
- **Local-first, always.** Your data is yours. Everything—from your history to your settings—is stored locally on your Mac using SwiftData. No accounts, no clouds, no tracking.
- **Deep Analytics.** Go beyond simple WPM charts. Visualize your key confidence, track daily streaks, and see exactly which transitions are holding you back through an elegant dashboard.
- **Tailored to your setup.** Whether you use QWERTY, Colemak, or Dvorak, the engine adapts. Customize everything from caret animations and background noise to mechanical switch sounds (Alpaca, Cream, or Clicky).

## Getting Started
### Requirements
- macOS 14.0 or later.
- Xcode 15.0+ (if building from source).

### Running the App
Open `Typa.xcodeproj` in Xcode and run the main target. The app uses SwiftData for persistence, which will initialize automatically on first launch.
